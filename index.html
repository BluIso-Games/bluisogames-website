About BluIso Games

We see the world at 30 degrees

BluIso Games is an independent studio devoted to isometric worlds — cities, dungeons, and heists rendered with the clarity of a blueprint and the warmth of a sunset. Founded by three level designers who couldn't stop tilting the camera, we build games where every rooftop, alley, and vault is exactly where you'd expect it to be — and hides something you wouldn't.

What we stand on

Three pillars, one grid

Readable depth

If a player can see it, they can reach it. Our worlds are honest — every ledge, shadow, and shortcut is real geometry, never painted scenery.

Craft over crunch

We ship when it's ready. A sustainable 48-person team has carried six titles across the line without a single cancelled weekend — and it shows in the work.

Players first, forever

No paywalled endings, no pay-to-win. Every major update we've released since 2017 has been free, and that's not changing.

The road so far

A brief history of the grid

2017

Three level designers rent one desk

BluIso Games is founded in a shared studio space with a single license of a game engine and a very strong opinion about camera angles.

Origin
2019

Gridlock ships and finds its people

Our debut puzzle title sells modestly, then explodes a year later when speedrunners discover the unintended diagonal-dash. We patch it in as a feature.

Milestone
2022

Vaultbreakers goes live

Our co-op heist game launches with 40,000 concurrent players on day one and becomes the studio's flagship live title.

Milestone
2026

Neon Delta announced

Our most ambitious project — a full open-world isometric city — enters pre-order alongside the launch of BluIso Publishing.
