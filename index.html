About BluIso Games

The BluIso Manifesto

Founded 2017. Forty-eight developers. One philosophy, written down so we can be held to it.

№ 1

Locked in… immediately.

We believe games should capture players from the very beginning. The first moments matter. The first mechanic matters. The first encounter matters. Players should feel excitement, curiosity, tension, or joy within minutes — not hours.

We reject the idea that games must become fun eventually.

№ 2

Action creates engagement.

Action is not limited to combat. Action is momentum. Decision-making. Tension. Discovery. Movement. Risk.

Whether it's surviving a slasher, chasing criminals, controlling elemental powers, or fighting with a severed hand, our games are built around active participation.

Players should always be doing, feeling, deciding, reacting.

№ 3

Fantasy comes first.

Every BluIso game begins with a question: what fantasy are we delivering?

The goal is not realism for its own sake. Complexity for its own sake. Systems for their own sake. The goal is making players feel like they are living a unique fantasy that only that game can provide.

№ 4

We respect player time.

We do not believe games need five hours to become interesting. We avoid unnecessary friction, excessive tutorials, bloated inventories, complicated build systems, and mechanics that distract from the core experience.

Every system should support the fantasy. Every mechanic should support engagement. Every minute should matter.

№ 5

Games should feel authored.

Our games are not designed by committee or trends. Each project should have a strong identity, a clear voice, and a point of view.

We strive to create games that feel handcrafted and intentional — experiences that could only come from BluIso.

№ 6

Variety is strength.

Genre does not define us. Our games may be shooters, horror games, action adventures, fantasy worlds, platformers, or experiences yet to be imagined.

The fantasy changes. The setting changes. The perspective changes. But our philosophy remains the same.

№ 7

We believe in thrills.

Games should make players lean forward. They should create excitement, tension, satisfaction, wonder, fear, adrenaline, curiosity, and joy.

We want players to sit down and quickly realize: "I need to see where this goes."

Our Mission

BluIso creates immediately engaging action experiences built around unique player fantasies — highly authored games that respect player time, prioritize excitement, and feel unmistakably BluIso.

A brief history of the house

2017

Three level designers rent one desk

BluIso Games is founded in a shared studio space with a single license of a game engine and a very strong opinion about first impressions.

Origin
2019

Gridlock ships and finds its people

Our debut puzzle title sells modestly, then explodes a year later when speedrunners discover the unintended diagonal-dash. We patch it in as a feature.

Milestone
2022

Vaultbreakers goes live

Our co-op heist game launches with 40,000 concurrent players on day one and becomes the studio's flagship live title.

Milestone
2026

Neon Delta announced

Our most ambitious project — a full open-world city — enters pre-order alongside the launch of BluIso Publishing.
