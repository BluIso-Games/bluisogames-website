A studio with a point of view

BluIso Games is an independent video game studio devoted to one perspective: the isometric view. It's the angle of dioramas and dollhouses, of strategy maps and city grids — the view that lets a player hold an entire world in their hands.

We founded the studio on a simple belief: small teams making deliberate choices can build worlds that feel bigger than their file size. Every tile in a BluIso game is placed by a person, every system designed to reward curiosity, and every world stitched through with a golden thread of wonder.

From the cozy frontier of Homestead Hex to the glowing caverns of Lantern Depths — and now the floating ruins of Cobalt Vale — we make games you can live inside for a while, and think about long after.

Handcrafted, always

No procedural filler. If a tile exists in our worlds, someone on the team put it there on purpose.

Depth over noise

Quiet systems that reward attention. Our games ask questions instead of shouting answers.

Players are guests

We build worlds worth visiting — no dark patterns, no manipulative economies, no wasted time.

The golden thread

Every BluIso game hides one strand of gold: a mystery that connects our worlds to each other.

Announcement · August 12, 2026 · BluIso Games

GAME ANNOUNCED: COBALT VALE

Today we're thrilled to announce our third and most ambitious world: Cobalt Vale. Set among the floating ruins of a kingdom that chose the sky over the ground, Cobalt Vale is an isometric odyssey about restoration — of places, of machines, and of the people who abandoned both.

You play the Vale's last Mason, armed with a chisel, a glider, and a ledger of everything the kingdom lost. Every block you place rewrites the world below: reroute a river on one island and a garden blooms on another. The Vale remembers everything you build.

Cobalt Vale has been in quiet development for eighteen months and enters full production this fall. It is targeting a 2027 release on PC and consoles. Sharp-eyed players of Lantern Depths may already suspect where the golden thread leads this time.

We'll be sharing development diaries right here on The Iso Informant every month until launch. Thank you, as always, for visiting our worlds.

— The BluIso Team

Dev Diary · July 3, 2026 · Engineering

How we light an isometric cavern

Lantern Depths had one rule: light is currency. Every candle, lantern, and glow-moss cluster in the game's 90 caverns is a hand-placed light source with a real cost to the player — which meant our lighting couldn't be an afterthought. It had to be the game.

Traditional isometric games fake their lighting with pre-baked tile sprites. We went the other way: every tile in Lantern Depths carries its own luminance value, updated in real time as your lantern dims. Shadows lengthen as your fuel burns. The dark isn't a filter — it's a simulation.

The hardest part was restraint. Early builds looked spectacular and played terribly; when everything glows, nothing matters. The final game cut our light sources by 60%, and every cavern got darker, quieter, and better.

If you've ever hesitated at the edge of your lantern's reach in cavern 44 — that hesitation took us two years to build.

— The BluIso Team

Studio · June 18, 2026 · BluIso Games

Homestead Hex passes one million settlers

One million of you have founded a town on the hex frontier. When we shipped Homestead Hex in 2022, our internal target was modest enough that we never wrote it down. This week, the game crossed one million players.

Some numbers we love: the average town survives its first winter with 4 hearths burning. The most-traded good, by an absurd margin, is honey. And 12% of you have found the golden hex — though far fewer understand what it means yet.

Homestead Hex will receive one more free content update later this year, bringing new frontier events and, for our most patient mayors, an answer to the question the golden hex has been asking since launch.

Thank you for keeping your hearths burning gold.

— The BluIso Team

Community · May 29, 2026 · Community Team

The golden thread: your best theories

Every BluIso game hides one strand of gold. A hex that hums in Homestead Hex. A lantern in Lantern Depths that never runs out. You've noticed — and the theory threads have gotten out of hand, in the best possible way.

Some of our favorites: that the golden thread is a river seen from three different worlds. That the lanternkeeper and the frontier's first mayor are the same person. That the thread isn't in the games at all, but in the order you play them.

We won't confirm anything. But we will say this: one theory posted this spring made the whole studio go quiet for a moment. Its author is closer than they know.

Keep pulling the thread. Cobalt Vale will give you more to work with soon.

— The BluIso Team
